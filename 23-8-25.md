Sum of squares of first N natural numbers - Python
The goal here is to calculate the sum of squares of the first n natural numbers. The sum of squares is the result of adding up the squares of each number from 1 to n.
For example, if n = 5, the sum of squares would be 12 + 22 + 32+ 42+52=55. Let's explore different methods to compute this sum efficiently.
Using for loop
This is the most traditional and beginner-friendly method. It uses a simple for loop to iterate from 1 to n, calculating the square of each number and adding it to the total.
